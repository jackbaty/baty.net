## Design Context

### Users
Jack Baty's personal blog. Has an audience of regular readers, but the site is primarily a personal record — journal entries, short notes, photography posts, and long-form writing. It should feel nice and readable without performing "crafted publication" energy. The reader is a thoughtful person who came here deliberately, not a skimmer.

### Brand Personality
Analog · Simplicity · Clean

The site covers film photography (Leica, darkroom work), Emacs, fountain pens, and the deliberate choice of slower, more tactile tools. The feeling should evoke a well-set book or a printed journal — not a tech blog, not a portfolio, not a magazine. Just a person writing carefully.

Tone is: unhurried, considered, a little quiet. Nothing shouty.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light-first, with a faithful dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`. The light theme is the primary experience — warm off-whites, not clinical whites.
- **Typography**: Body text in **Vollkorn** (warm, bookish serif — designed for long-form screen reading, not overused). UI, navigation, and metadata in **Hanken Grotesk** (clean contemporary grotesque, pairs well with Vollkorn's warmth without fighting it).
- **Color accent**: Warm amber/honey — reminiscent of old ink, developed film, aged paper. Something like `oklch(0.68 0.13 72)`. Not blue. Not corporate. Muted enough to feel analog.
- **Anti-references**: Nothing that reads as a tech blog, SaaS product, or "AI made this." No cyan-on-dark. No gradients. No dashboards.

### Design Principles

1. **Content is the design.** The typography and spacing should serve the writing and photographs. Decorative elements earn their place or they don't appear.

2. **Warm restraint.** Minimal doesn't mean cold. The off-whites should feel like good paper; the spacing should feel like breathing room, not sterile emptiness.

3. **Analog cues, not nostalgia.** The palette and type can evoke print without being retro. No sepia filters, no faux-distressed textures. Just careful choices that feel timeless.

4. **Nothing competes with the photographs.** Images are a major content type. The surrounding chrome should step back when a photo is present.

5. **Consistent but not rigid.** Posts, journal entries, and notes have distinct characters. The design system should accommodate all three without forcing them into the same mold.
