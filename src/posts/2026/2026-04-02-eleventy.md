Hugo and I go way back, and our relationship has been tumultuous at times. Sometimes I get mad at it for introducing yet another breaking change. Sometimes I'm bored with it. Sometimes I get frustrated with the way things work (or don't). All three of those came up for me this week, so I did something about it.

Rather than fix what was broken, I completely revamped site using [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev). I've made passes at it like this before, but this time I started from scratch and, with a bit of robot help, made it about as simple as I could.

All the content has been migrated. I've tried to keep URLs the same as before. Post and Journal URLs should be good. Notes might break, though, sorry.

I'm missing a few things yet, but I'm started to lose momentum today. I did all this in half a day, but I need a break. I'm going to deploy it, since that will motivate me to finish.

It's deliberately simple, theme-wise. I may have gone too far by include all content types on the home page, but we'll see how it settles in.

Let me know if you hate it or if anything is broken.
