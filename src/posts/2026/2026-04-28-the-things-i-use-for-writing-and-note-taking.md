Do you know why have trouble finding things? Here's why...


![](/img/2026/20260427-dock-1.jpg)


<!-- more -->

-   [**Ghostty**](https://ghostty.org/?ref=copingmechanism.com). TUIs and CLI tools never get old. And I still like using Vim as $EDITOR
-   [**Obsidian**](https://obsidian.md/?ref=copingmechanism.com). My resistance to using Obsidian is sometimes overcome by its utility, ubiquity, and ease of use.
-   [**Emacs**](https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/?ref=copingmechanism.com). My everything tool for the past decade. It's sometimes a problem for me.
-   [**BBEdit**](https://www.barebones.com/products/bbedit/?ref=copingmechanism.com). The venerable text editor that remains unmatched in utility and stability. It indeed doesn't suck.
-   [**Tinderbox**](https://eastgate.com/Tinderbox/index.html?ref=copingmechanism.com). Deep, powerful outlining, visual mapping, and general note wizardry. A favorite since 2004.
-   [**Bear**](https://bear.app/?ref=copingmechanism.com). The nicest, cleanest non-plain-text note app I know. It's so slick, and updates are gradual and deliberate.
-   **Apple Notes**. It's always there when I need it, even though I don't love it.

You can understand why I love using these. They're all great, in their own ways. Which one I'm interested in at any moment depends on my mood. Hence, I have trouble finding things. Having access to so many wonderful tools is both a blessing and a curse.
